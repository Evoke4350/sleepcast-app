First public F-Droid build.

- Sleep-timer podcast player: 5, 45, 60 minutes, or all night
- Gentle native volume fade before it stops
- Per-feed volume leveling
- Three ways to pick a night: shuffle, spread, or a varied mix
- All-night mode auto-advances through the lineup
- Leave the player and keep listening; a now-playing banner brings you back
- Optional get-up nudge after a restless spell
- Add feeds by URL or OPML import/export
